GROT-X — Privacy Policy

Version 1.1 · Last updated: 8 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains — in accordance with Articles 13 and 14 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) — how personal data is processed when you use GROT-X (web and mobile application, the "App"), a training assistant for archers.

2. What Data We Process — Purposes and Legal Bases

2.1 Account and Sign-In Data

When you register, we process your e-mail address and password (stored only as a cryptographic hash by Firebase Authentication). Alternatively you can sign in with your Google account, in which case Google provides us with your e-mail address and display name.

Purpose: providing your user account. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract).

2.2 Profile Data

Data you provide voluntarily: name or display name, date of birth, gender, club affiliation (name, city), bow type and equipment data (sight settings, arrows).

Purpose: calculating age-appropriate distances and target faces under World Archery rules, personalising your training. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

2.3 Training Data

Training sessions, arrow plots, scores, statistics, notes, tournament results, calendar entries and derived values (experience points, level, handicap).

Purpose: the App's core function — recording and analysing your training. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

2.4 Coach–Athlete Feature

If you explicitly accept a coach invitation, the coach gains read-only access to your training data (sessions, statistics, tournaments) and can leave notes and messages. Your private notes remain inaccessible to coaches. You can disconnect at any time in the settings; access ends immediately.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent, revocable at any time by disconnecting).

2.5 Online Battles and World Leaderboard

If you take part in online duels ("Battles") or the world leaderboard, your display name, level and results are visible to other signed-in users. Participation is voluntary.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (provision of the feature you use).

2.6 Location Data (Weather)

To display weather conditions, the App — only after you grant permission via your browser/operating system — requests your approximate location once. The coordinates are transmitted solely to the weather service Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to retrieve temperature and wind. We do not store your coordinates; only the temperature and wind values attached to a training session are saved.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent via the location permission; revocable at any time in your device settings).

2.7 Camera (QR Scanner and Video Mirror)

The App uses the camera only after you grant permission, for: (a) scanning QR codes (connecting with a coach) and (b) the "Delay Mirror" feature (video technique analysis). Video recordings remain exclusively on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

2.8 Technical Data (Hosting, Security)

When you use the App, our hosting provider (Firebase Hosting) processes technically necessary data in server logs: IP address, time of access and browser information. To protect against abuse and automated traffic we use Firebase App Check with reCAPTCHA v3 (Google), which transmits device and interaction signals to Google.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the secure and stable operation of the App).

2.9 Local Storage

The App stores technically necessary data locally on your device (e.g. the training session in progress, language preference, an offline cache of the database). We do not use tracking cookies or any advertising or analytics trackers.

4. Retention Period

Your data is stored for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time directly in the App (Settings → Profile → Delete account); your profile and training data are then erased without undue delay. Hosting server logs are deleted automatically according to Firebase's standard retention periods.

5. Your Rights

  • Access (Art. 15 GDPR) — in the App: "Export my data"
  • Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR) — you can edit profile data yourself
  • Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR) — account deletion directly in the App
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR) — JSON export in the App
  • Objection (Art. 21 GDPR) to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdrawal of consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR) with effect for the future

An informal e-mail to info@aves-24.de is sufficient.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR) in your EU member state of residence.

6. Minors

The App is intended for archers of all age categories, including children and youth (the World Archery classes cover the youngest athletes). Independent use of the App is permitted from the age of 16 — the digital age of consent that applies in Germany and Poland (Art. 8 GDPR). Some other EU member states set a lower age (down to 13); we apply 16 as a single threshold, which is compliant in all of them.

If an athlete is under 16, the App requires the consent of a parent or legal guardian before it can be used. We determine age from the date of birth provided; if it indicates a person under 16, the App shows a screen on which the parent/guardian confirms consent and provides their email address. That address and the consent record (policy version, date) are stored as evidence of consent — this serves the reasonable efforts to verify consent required by Art. 8(2) GDPR. The guardian's email address is visible only to the account owner and the administrator.

The child's date of birth is stored in the private part of the profile and is not shared with coaches. A coach only sees the derived age category (e.g. "Jugend"), which is needed to select competition distances and target faces.

A parent or guardian may withdraw consent at any time by deleting the child's account in the App (Settings → Profile → Delete account) or by contacting us at info@aves-24.de.

7. No Automated Decision-Making

We do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR. Training analyses are provided solely for your own information.

8. Data Security

All connections are TLS-encrypted. Access to your data is protected by server-side security rules (Firestore Security Rules): other users cannot access your training data unless you explicitly grant access (coach invitation).

9. Changes to This Policy

We update this policy when features or legal requirements change. The current version is always available at this address; we will inform you of material changes within the App.
